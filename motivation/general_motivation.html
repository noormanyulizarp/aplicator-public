Why I'm Seeking a New Opportunity (General Statement)

After 10 years in QA across fintech, edtech, and streaming platforms, I have watched established companies move slowly on modern testing practices. Most recently, I managed end-to-end quality for GoPay's financial features serving over 30 million users. In my experience, many companies still resist adopting early-stage testing and smart automation, even when these approaches clearly reduce release risks.

What draws me to Shine is the exact opposite. Shine is a tech-forward fintech that has scaled to over 450,000 businesses across Europe using modern tools, which shows an engineering culture that readily adopts new and effective approaches. As a QA Analyst in the Porto Product & Technology team, I want to bring my full experience to help solve the testing challenges of scaling a fintech product across multiple European markets. My background ranges from deep manual testing to building automation platforms adopted by over 50 engineers at Ruangguru, as well as integrating AI tools to streamline testing workflows.

Specifically, I want to help build efficient, highly automated testing systems that allow a small QA team to achieve massive results. As Shine grows from 450,000 to millions of businesses, this is exactly the challenge I am looking for.
