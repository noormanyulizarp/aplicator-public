B2B Contractor Eligibility — Indonesia Context

What "B2B contractor" means in Indonesia

To work as a B2B contractor for Bonapolia (a Portuguese company) from Indonesia, Noorman would need:

  1. NPWP (Indonesian tax ID) — already required for any formal income
  2. Business entity OR freelancer status:
  3. PT Perorangan (individual limited company) — easiest, ~1 week to register via OSS system, costs ~500K IDR
  4. CV (commanditaire vennootschap) — partnership, more complex
  5. Freelancer status (non-formal) — possible but less ideal for international contracts
  6. Tax treaty awareness: Indonesia-Portugal tax treaty exists (signed 2018) — may reduce withholding tax

Recommended approach for Noorman

  • Form PT Perorangan (simplest) — register via Online Single Submission (OSS)
  • Use this entity to invoice Bonapolia monthly
  • Open a USD business bank account (e.g. via Wise, Aspire, or local bank USD account) for receiving EUR payments
  • Bonapolia handles the MSA + NDA paperwork

Estimated timeline to be B2B-ready

  • 1-2 weeks to form PT Perorangan (if not already done)

  • Total: ~2-3 weeks to be fully operational as B2B contractor
