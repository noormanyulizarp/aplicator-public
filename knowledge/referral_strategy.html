Referral Strategy — Your Strongest Apply Lever

A referral increases your interview chance by 5-10x compared to cold application. Most engineers underuse this.

Why referrals work

  • Recruiters triage referral apps first (they're pre-vetted)
  • Often skip ATS keyword filtering entirely
  • Internal recruiter incentives: many companies pay $2-5k referral bonuses
  • Even a weak referral (someone you met once) > cold application

Where to find referrals

Tier 1 — Strong (current/former close colleagues)

  • Former Ruangguru teammates
  • Former Vidio teammates
  • Current GoTo colleagues (for other BUs)
  • Former Jenius colleagues
  • Reach out: WhatsApp/Telegram, brief message, ask politely

Tier 2 — Medium (LinkedIn 2nd-degree connections)

  • Search LinkedIn for: <Company> + engineer + Indonesia
  • Filter by mutual connections
  • Ask mutual connection to introduce you

Tier 3 — Cold outreach (cold LinkedIn DM)

  • Find engineers or recruiters at the company
  • Send a brief, value-first message
  • Expect ~10% response rate; that's fine

How to ask for a referral

Template — close colleague

Hi [Name]! 👋

Saw that [Company] is hiring for [Role] — looks like a strong match for me given my
[1-sentence relevant skill/background].

Would you be open to referring me? Totally fine if not — no pressure either way.

JD link: [URL]
My CV: [Google Drive link or attached]

Either way, let's catch up soon — coffee on me next time!
Noorman

Template — LinkedIn 2nd-degree (mutual intro)

Hi [Mutual Connection],

Hope you're well! I noticed you're connected with [Name] at [Company]. I'm exploring
[Role] opportunities there — my background is [1-line]. Would you feel comfortable
introducing me? Totally fine if you'd rather not.

Either way, thanks for keeping in touch!
Noorman

Template — cold LinkedIn DM to engineer at target company

Etiquette

  • ✅ Always personalize — no copy-paste to 10 people at once
  • ✅ Include the JD link — saves them time
  • ✅ Make it easy to say no — "no pressure" disarms awkwardness
  • ✅ Thank them regardless of outcome

  • ✅ If you get hired via their referral, send a thank-you gift (lunch, coffee, small token)

  • ❌ Don't spam multiple people at the same company simultaneously — they'll talk

  • ❌ Don't ask someone you've never talked to for a referral — build rapport first
  • ❌ Don't expect a yes — referrals are a favor, not a transaction
  • ❌ Don't go silent after they refer you — keep them posted on status

Referral pipeline — long-term play

Build your referral network BEFORE you need it:

  • Quarterly: message 5 ex-colleagues, ask how they're doing
  • Annually: write a LinkedIn post sharing something you learned — strengthens your network
  • Always: when someone reaches out to YOU for referral, take it seriously — karma compounds
