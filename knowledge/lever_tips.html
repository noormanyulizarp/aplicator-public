Lever Apply — Workflow & Tips

Lever is the second most common ATS in tech (used by Notion, Ramp, Plaid, etc.). Simpler than Greenhouse — usually just CV + 1-2 questions.

Why Lever is great

  • ✅ Fastest apply flow of any ATS (often <2 min)
  • ✅ Almost always auto-acknowledges with confirmation email
  • ✅ Often has a single form across all roles at the company
  • ✅ Mobile-friendly — you can apply on the go

Lever workflow

Step-by-step (target: 2 min per app)

  1. Open the role on the company's Lever page (often jobs.lever.co/<company>)
  2. Click "Apply for this job"
  3. Personal info: name, email, phone — autofill from browser
  4. CV upload: drag your tailored PDF
  5. Optional: cover letter — paste from CL_Template_<Role>.docx
  6. Custom questions (if any): usually 1-2 — salary expectation, work authorization
  7. Links: paste LinkedIn + GitHub + portfolio
  8. Submit

Lever-specific tips

  • "My Applications" page: jobs.lever.co/me/applications — track all your Lever apps in one place
  • Status feedback is faster than Greenhouse — usually within 2-5 days
  • Email confirmation always comes — if you don't get one in 5 min, check spam or re-submit
  • Custom questions are minimal — Lever's philosophy is "let the CV speak"
  • Many Lever companies don't require cover letters — submit anyway if Tier 1

When Lever asks custom questions

Common patterns: - "Are you legally authorized to work in [country]?" → Yes/No - "Will you now or in the future require visa sponsorship?" → Yes/No - "What's your expected salary?" → range in local currency - "How did you hear about this role?" → referral name if any

Gotchas

  • ❌ Lever's "My Applications" page sometimes doesn't sync statuses — keep your own tracker
  • ❌ Some companies add a screening question with a hidden "right answer" (e.g. "What HTTP status code means...") — answer carefully
  • ❌ If you apply twice to the same company in 30 days, the second usually overwrites the first (not always — varies)
