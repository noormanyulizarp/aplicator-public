Greenhouse Apply — Workflow & Tips

Greenhouse is the most common ATS in tech (used by Stripe, Notion, GitLab, etc.). Forms are usually consistent across companies — once you learn the pattern, applications take 3-5 min.

Why Greenhouse matters

  • Many Tier 1 companies use it (Stripe, Airbnb, Coinbase, Doordash, etc.)
  • Forms are usually identical — autofill works great
  • Often requires custom questions (work authorization, salary, referral source)

Setup (one-time)

1. Browser autofill

  • Chrome: Settings → Autofill → add your name, email, phone, address
  • Or use a password manager (Bitwarden, 1Password) that auto-fills forms
  • Or install Simplify extension — purpose-built for job applications

2. Pre-write common answers

Save these in knowledge/answers/ so you can copy-paste:

  • Work authorization: "I am an Indonesian citizen authorized to work in Indonesia. I do not require sponsorship for Indonesia-based roles."
  • Salary expectation: "Open to discussion. Based on market research, my expected range is IDR XX-XX M / month for [role]."
  • Why this company?: keep a template; personalize per app
  • Referral source: "Referred by [Name], [Title] at [Company]"
  • Years of experience: "10 years in software engineering, with focus on [role]"

3. CV file naming

Keep a clean filename for upload — recruiters see it: - CV_Noorman_SDET.pdf (good) - Resume_v3_FINAL.pdf (bad)

Greenhouse workflow

Step-by-step (target: 3-5 min per app)

  1. Open the role on the company's Greenhouse page (often <company>.greenhouse.io)
  2. Click "Apply" — opens the multi-step form
  3. Step 1: Personal info — autofill (name, email, phone, LinkedIn)
  4. Step 2: CV upload — drag your tailored PDF
  5. Step 3: Cover letter (if required) — paste from CL_Template_<Role>.docx (already personalized)
  6. Step 4: Custom questions — copy-paste from knowledge/answers/
  7. Step 5: Referral source — if referral, name the person; else "LinkedIn" / "Company website"
  8. Step 6: Diversity survey — optional, your choice
  9. Submit — you'll get an email confirmation

Custom question patterns to expect

Question type Example How to answer
Years of experience "How many years of Python experience?" Round down (honest), e.g. "8 years"
Work authorization "Are you authorized to work in [country]?" Yes/No with brief explanation
Salary expectation "What's your expected salary?" Range, currency, period
Why this company? "Why do you want to work at [Company]?" 2-3 sentences, mention a specific product/feature/news
Referral source "How did you hear about this role?" Name + relationship if referral
Availability "When can you start?" "Available from [date]" or "X weeks notice"

Greenhouse-specific tips

  • "My Applications" page: every Greenhouse-hosted company has one at <company>.greenhouse.io — track your status
  • Status meanings:
  • "Application Submitted" → in queue, not yet reviewed
  • "Application Review" → recruiter is reading
  • "Interview" → moved to next stage
  • "Hired" / "Rejected" → terminal states
  • Custom questions are mandatory — leaving blank = auto-reject
  • Referral source is weighted heavily — if you have a referral, ALWAYS name them
  • Some companies add "additional questions" after the first screen — check email

Referral flow on Greenhouse

If you have a referral: 1. Referrer submits you via internal portal — they fill a brief form 2. You receive an email with a personalized apply link 3. Apply via that link — your application is tagged as referral (priority queue) 4. Always thank your referrer — even if rejected

Common gotchas

  • ❌ Submitting without a cover letter when it's "optional but recommended" → submit one anyway for Tier 1
  • ❌ Forgetting to swap your CV variant (applied Backend role with QA CV)
  • ❌ Salary expectations too high (auto-reject) or too low (anchor low for negotiation)
  • ❌ Applying to multiple roles at the same company simultaneously (looks unfocused)
