LinkedIn Easy Apply — Workflow & Tips

What is Easy Apply?

LinkedIn Easy Apply lets you apply with 1-3 clicks using a pre-saved profile + CV. Lower friction, but also lower signal — recruiters see these as lower quality than direct Greenhouse/Lever applications.

When to use Easy Apply

  • ✅ Quick volume applications when you have a tailored CV ready
  • ✅ Companies you don't have a referral to (otherwise go referral route)
  • ✅ Exploring market / testing CV response rates

When NOT to use Easy Apply

  • ❌ For Tier 1 dream companies — apply directly via their site or referral
  • ❌ When the JD specifically asks for a cover letter / portfolio link
  • ❌ When the form asks questions your profile can't auto-fill

Setup (one-time)

1. Profile completeness

LinkedIn weights Easy Apply results by profile completeness. Fill in: - [ ] Headline (use your role-targeted tagline, e.g. "Senior SDET | Test Infrastructure @ GoPay | Ex-Ruangguru, Vidio") - [ ] About section (paste from Summary/<Role>/LinkedIn_Summary_<Role>.md) - [ ] Work experience — fill ALL 10 years - [ ] Skills section — add 30+ (matches ATS keyword list) - [ ] Featured section — pin a project / GitHub / article - [ ] Open to work — set to "Recruiters only" (not the green frame)

2. CV upload

  • Upload 4 CV variants (Backend, AI, QA, SDET) to LinkedIn
  • Set the most relevant one as default for the role you're targeting this week
  • Tip: rename files like CV_Noorman_SDET_GoPay.pdf — recruiters see the filename

3. Easy Apply pre-fill

When you click Easy Apply for the first time, LinkedIn asks: - Years of experience → fill in 10 - Work authorization → Yes (Indonesia citizen, no sponsorship needed for ID roles) - Willing to relocate → Yes / No depending on your preference - Salary expectation → set realistic range (research Glassdoor first)

Save these — they pre-fill future Easy Apply apps.

Easy Apply workflow

Per-app (target: 60 seconds)

  1. Open JD — read the first 3 lines of "Requirements"
  2. Decide: backend / AI / QA / SDET CV?
  3. Click "Easy Apply"
  4. Verify the right CV is attached (swap if needed)
  5. Answer any custom questions (pre-fill if asked before)
  6. Click submit
  7. Copy the JD URL — paste into apply_tracker.py add --link

Custom question pre-fill

LinkedIn saves your last answer. Common questions to expect: - "How many years of experience with [skill]?" → answer conservatively (round down) - "Are you authorized to work in [country]?" → Yes - "Will you now or in the future require sponsorship?" → No (for Indonesia roles) - "What's your expected salary?" → research first; for ID tech roles, typical range: - Senior SDET: IDR 25-45M/month - Senior Backend: IDR 30-50M/month - Senior AI Engineer: IDR 35-60M/month

Hidden gotchas

  • Don't apply to 100+ in a day — LinkedIn may flag as spam and limit your account
  • Easy Apply responses go to the same ATS as direct applications — quality > quantity
  • Some "Easy Apply" buttons route to company site anyway — check the form before submitting
  • Update your default CV when you tailor it — or you'll apply with the wrong variant

Tools to make Easy Apply faster

  • LinkedIn Premium: not strictly needed for Easy Apply, but InMail credits help for recruiter outreach
  • Browser extension: SimplifyJobs / LazyApply: auto-fills beyond LinkedIn (use with caution)
  • Bookmark your search URLs: https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/search/?f_AL=true&keywords=sdet&location=Indonesia&f_WT=2 (Easy Apply filter = f_AL=true)
