Workday / Company Site Apply — Workflow & Tips

Workday is the most painful ATS in tech (used by many large enterprises — banks, telcos, Fortune 500). Plan 15-30 min per application.

Why Workday is painful

  • ❌ Re-asks for everything already in your CV (work history, education, etc.)
  • ❌ Often requires manual account creation with a password
  • ❌ Multi-page form with mandatory fields
  • ❌ No autofill extension works perfectly
  • ❌ Often errors out without saving progress

When to use Workday

  • Only for Tier 1 dream companies that use it (rare in tech, common in banking/enterprise)
  • Otherwise: avoid — high effort, low signal

Setup before applying

1. Create a plain-text work history file

Save as knowledge/work_history.txt:

=== GoTo Financial (GoPay) ===
Title: Software Engineer in Test
Period: Jan 2023 — Present
Location: Jakarta, Indonesia
Salary: [leave blank or "competitive"]
Manager: [Name if you want to share]
Reason for leaving: N/A (current)
Duties:
- [bullets from your CV]

=== Ruangguru ===
... etc.

Workday will ask for this — having it pre-written saves 5+ minutes per app.

2. Education file

=== Jenderal Soedirman University ===
Degree: Bachelor of Software Engineering
Period: 2012 — 2016
GPA: 3.51
Location: Purwokerto, Indonesia

3. References

Some Workday apps ask for 2-3 references upfront. Prepare: - Name, title, company, email, phone, relationship - Ask permission before listing anyone

Workday workflow

Per-app (target: 15-30 min)

  1. Create account if first time at this company
  2. Save password in password manager
  3. Section 1: Personal info — autofill if possible, otherwise copy-paste
  4. Section 2: Work experience — paste from work_history.txt
  5. Section 3: Education — paste from above
  6. Section 4: Skills — paste from ATS keywords list
  7. Section 5: CV upload — attach tailored PDF
  8. Section 6: Custom questions — usually diversity survey, work authorization
  9. Section 7: References — fill if asked
  10. Section 8: Review & submit — check carefully, often errors at this step

Pro tips

  • Save often — Workday sometimes times out and loses everything
  • Use a dedicated email — Workday sends a LOT of follow-up emails
  • One account per company — different companies have different Workday instances
  • Take screenshots of confirmation pages — sometimes the email never comes
  • Apply on desktop — Workday mobile is unusable

Alternative: skip Workday entirely

If the company also accepts applications via: - LinkedIn (apply via Easy Apply + link back) - Email to recruiter (find recruiter on LinkedIn, send direct) - Referral (your strongest lever for Workday-using companies)

→ Use the alternative. Workday is rarely worth the time unless it's a Tier 1 company.
